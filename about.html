About Val

I’m a senior HR and hiring consultant with over a decade of experience supporting startups and small businesses. I work with founders and leadership teams who need experienced HR support that is directly tied to how the business operates and where it is trying to go.

At its core, HR should support the company’s goals. My work is focused on helping teams hire effectively, operate smoothly, and make people decisions that enable growth rather than slow it down.

How I work

I take a practical, business-first approach to HR. That means understanding your priorities, constraints, and operating model, then aligning HR work to support them.

  • Focusing on work that has a clear business impact
  • Prioritizing based on what the team needs now, not what “should” exist on paper
  • Building processes that leaders and managers will actually use
  • Staying involved long enough to ensure changes are adopted and working

Clients typically engage with me on a project basis or as a fractional HR partner.

Experience & background

I’ve worked across early-stage startups and small businesses in periods of growth and change. My experience includes:

  • Fractional HR leadership
  • Full-cycle recruiting
  • HR operations and process design
  • HR systems and PEO implementations

I’m SHRM-CP certified and a Top-Rated Plus freelancer on Upwork, with long-term client relationships built on trust and repeat work.

Who I work best with

  • Founder-led companies
  • Lean teams without internal HR
  • Leaders who want HR support that is grounded in business realities
